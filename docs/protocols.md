The [Protocols/ directory on `main`](https://github.com/Schizo-Studios-509/SoundCraft-Master-Hub/tree/main/Protocols) is the authoritative source for SoundCraft protocols. [Archived protocols](https://github.com/Schizo-Studios-509/SoundCraft-Master-Hub/tree/main/Protocols/Archives) are retained separately for reference.

Published protocol pages should identify their version, status, effective date, and dependencies. Archived and superseded documents must remain explicitly labeled.

**Important:** Existing audio-stamp documents contain differing durations and specifications. Do not infer that an older protocol is current without checking the controlling revision and associated asset.
